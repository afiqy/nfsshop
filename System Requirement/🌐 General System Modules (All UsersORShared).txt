🌐 General System Modules (All Users / Shared)

1. Authentication & Access Control
   - Secure login with password policies, CAPTCHA after repeated failures, and account lockouts.
   - Optional SSO (Google/Microsoft) and multi-factor authentication for high-privilege actions.
   - Role-based permissions (Superadmin, Owner, Staff, Client) with session timeouts and device management.

2. User Account & Profile Management
   - Update personal details, contact information, avatars/logos, and preferred communication channels.
   - Manage passwords, recovery options, and linked authentication methods (SSO, MFA devices).
   - Configure personal preferences such as language, timezone, and notification settings.

3. Dashboard & Quick Actions
   - Personalized landing page per role highlighting KPIs, pending tasks, and upcoming appointments.
   - Configurable widgets and shortcuts for frequent actions (create invoice, schedule job, add vehicle).
   - Consolidated activity feed showing recent changes, notifications, and reminders.

4. Notifications & Alerts
   - Multi-channel delivery (email, SMS, WhatsApp, in-app) with reusable templates and localization.
   - Triggers include bookings, invoice/payment status, stock thresholds, and system announcements.
   - Delivery tracking, opt-in/out controls, quiet hours, and escalation rules for critical alerts.

5. Global Search & Smart Filters
   - Unified search across clients, vehicles, jobs, invoices, inventory, payments, and documents.
   - Advanced filters, saved views, partial/fuzzy matching, and search by identifiers (plate, phone, invoice no.).
   - Export filtered results to CSV/PDF when allowed by role permissions.

6. Document & Media Library
   - Upload, tag, and categorize files with virus scanning and file-type/size validation.
   - Attach documents to jobs, invoices, vehicles, and client profiles with version history.
   - Preview common file types and enforce storage quotas or retention rules by subscription tier.

7. Audit Trail & Activity Monitoring
   - Capture create/update/delete events with timestamp, user, origin (web/mobile/API), and old/new values.
   - Filterable audit log with export, retention policies, and tamper-evident storage.
   - Real-time alerts for suspicious activity such as repeated failed logins or access from new devices.

8. Localization & Regional Settings
   - Multi-language UI, currency formatting, tax rules, and measurement units configurable per workshop.
   - Timezone-aware scheduling for bookings, reminders, and invoice due dates.
   - Support for government-compliant numbering (invoice prefixes, tax IDs) per locale.

9. Support & Helpdesk
   - In-app support widget to submit tickets, attach screenshots/documents, and track resolution status.
   - Knowledge base, FAQs, and interactive guides accessible to all roles.
   - Feedback forms and satisfaction surveys routed to Superadmin support teams.

10. Mobile & Multi-Device Experience
    - Responsive layouts optimized for phones/tablets with streamlined workflows for workshop floors.
    - Native/PWA capabilities: push notifications, camera uploads, signature capture, barcode scanning.
    - Offline-ready capture for key tasks (job check-ins, photos) with background sync when online.

11. Data Import & Export
    - Bulk import templates for clients, vehicles, inventory, price lists, and historical invoices.
    - Export reports and records (invoices, payments, job cards) to CSV/PDF or connected accounting apps.
    - Scheduled exports/backups aligned with subscription tiers and compliance requirements.
