👑 Superadmin (System Owner)

1. User Management (Workshop Owners)
   - Create, edit, suspend, or delete workshop owner accounts.
   - Reset passwords, manage roles & permissions (Owner, Staff, Client).
   - Impersonate owner (login as) for troubleshooting.

2. Workshop Management
   - View/manage all workshops.
   - Configure workshop details (address, services, staff, pricing, taxes).
   - Monitor workshop activity & performance.

3. Billing & Subscription Management
   - Manage subscription plans (monthly/yearly fees for workshop owners).
   - Track payments, invoices, and overdue accounts.
   - Integration with payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal, etc.).
   - Generate invoices for workshop owners.

4. Item & Service Catalog (Global Settings)
   - Define global product/service categories.
   - Option to push standard pricing/service templates to workshops.
   - Owners can override but Superadmin can enforce defaults.

5. Analytics & Reporting
   - Revenue reports (per workshop, overall).
   - Invoices generated/paid status across all workshops.
   - Top services/products across workshops.
   - Active users, growth trends, churn rate.

6. System Settings
   - Global tax settings, currencies, localization.
   - Email/SMS templates for notifications.
   - API integrations (e.g., accounting software, WhatsApp notifications).
   - Backup & restore options.

7. Access & Audit Logs
   - Track logins, failed logins, suspicious activities.
   - Activity history of all users (who created/edited/deleted records).

8. Support & Communication
   - Manage support tickets from workshop owners.
   - System-wide announcements & notifications.
   - Internal messaging with admins.

9. Client (End-user) Management (Optional)
   - View all clients registered across workshops.
   - Handle data privacy (GDPR/PDPA compliance).
   - Manage blacklists (fraudulent clients).

10. Superadmin Dashboard
   - High-level KPIs: active workshops, revenue, pending invoices, system usage.
   - Quick actions (approve new workshops, check payment failures, etc.).

11. Vehicle Management
    - Can view/search all vehicles across workshops.
    - Analytics: most serviced brands/models, mileage patterns, etc.
    - (Usually no need to add/edit vehicles, but can for testing/support).
