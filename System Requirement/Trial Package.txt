📑 Refined Free Trial & Trial Run Requirements
1. 🎟️ Free Trial (1 Month)
 - Eligibility & Uniqueness Checks (same as before):
     - Unique workshop name (primary).
     - Unique owner email.
     - Unique address (secondary check).
     - Re-registering with same workshop name under new email = staff role (no new trial).
 - Trial Duration: 30 days.
 - After Expiry Behavior:
     - Owner/Admin login →
         - Redirected to Dashboard, but all modules (jobs, invoices, etc.) are grayed out/disabled.
         - Only Subscription Page remains active to upgrade/pay.
     - Staff login →
         - Redirected to “Subscription Required” notice page.
         - Message: “Your workshop’s subscription has expired. Please contact your workshop admin.”
         - Staff cannot make payments or manage subscription.
 - Notifications:
     - Welcome email at registration.
     - Reminder at 7 days before expiry.
     - Final reminder on expiry day.


2. ⏳ Trial Run (3 Months, 50% Off)

 - Eligibility: One-time per workshop.
 - Pricing: 50% discount on quarterly plan.
 - Subscription Handling:
     - Full access during 3 months.
     - Auto-renew at full quarterly price when trial ends.
 - Cancellation Rules:
     - User may cancel early → no refund, but access continues until end date.
 - After Expiry Behavior:
     - Same as Free Trial: system still opens to dashboard, but only subscription page is usable.
     - Staff users see “Subscription Required” notice.
 - Notifications:
     - Confirmation email when activated.
     - Reminder 7 days before expiry.
     - Reminder 1 day before auto-renew.


3. 🔒 Fraud Prevention
 - Enforce uniqueness on:
     - Workshop name (primary).
     - Owner email.
     - Workshop address (secondary).
 - If duplicate workshop name/address is detected:
     - Register user as staff under existing workshop.


4. ⚙️ System Logic
 - Free Trial (no payment, no auto-renew).
     - Expiry → lock features, show subscription page only.
 - Trial Run (3 months, paid).
     - Expiry → auto-convert to quarterly subscription unless canceled.
     - Cancellation → no refund, access until end date, then lock to subscription page.
 - Staff behavior after expiry:
     - Blocked from system features.
     - Only see “Contact Admin” message.



5. 📂 Data Fields
Extend subscription table with:
 - trial_type (free_trial, trial_run, none)
 - trial_start_date
 - trial_end_date
 - subscription_status (active, expired, canceled)
 - auto_renew (true/false, applies to trial_run & paid subs)
 - workshop_name (unique)
 - workshop_address (unique check secondary)
