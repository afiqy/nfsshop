**👨‍🔧 Workshop Staff (Employees)**
Staff role should have limited access:

1. Job Management
   - View & update assigned jobs/orders.
   - Record service notes & progress.
   - Log parts used from inventory.

2. Invoices (optional based on permission)
   - Generate invoices for completed jobs.
   - Mark payments received (cash, POS, transfer).

3. Client Interaction
   - Check client details & history.
   - Confirm appointments.
   - Update vehicle/service records.

4. Dashboard (personal)
   - Today’s assigned jobs.
   - Pending tasks & deadlines.

5. Vehicle Management
  - View and update vehicle details while performing jobs.
  - Record mileage, condition, parts used.
  - Add service notes tied to vehicle history.
