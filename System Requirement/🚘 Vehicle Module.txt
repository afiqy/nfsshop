🔧 Features of Vehicle Module

1. Vehicle Master Data
   - Make, model, year, VIN/chassis number, engine number.
   - Plate/license number.
   - Color, mileage, fuel type.

2. Ownership & Linking
   - Each vehicle is tied to a Client account.
   - If client not registered, the Workshop Admin/Staff can still create a vehicle record and assign it later.
   - Allow multiple vehicles per client.

3. Service History
   - Every invoice, work order, or service done is logged against the vehicle.
   - Easy lookup: "Show me all services done on this car".

4. Reminders & Notifications
   - Service reminders (oil change, inspections, tire rotation).
   - Registration/insurance renewal reminders (if owner enables).

5. Attachments
   - Upload documents: registration papers, insurance, photos of damage before/after.

6. Search & Reports
   - Search by plate number, VIN, or client name.
   - Reports of vehicle types serviced (for workshop analytics).
