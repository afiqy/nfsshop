** 🚗 Client (Customer) **
(Client can be optional to register — so self-service features are optional.)

1. Profile & Account (if registered)
   - Manage personal details.
   - Store vehicle info.
   - Track service history.

2. Appointments
   - Book/cancel/reschedule services.
   - Receive reminders/notifications.

3. Invoices & Payments
   - View past invoices.
   - Pay online (if enabled).

4. Download receipts.
   - Loyalty / Rewards (optional feature)
   - See accumulated points/discounts.

5. Client Dashboard
   - Upcoming appointments.
   - Outstanding invoices.
   - Vehicle service timeline.

6. Vehicle Management
   - Optionally add/manage their own vehicles. (either already sold or something)
   - View service history of each vehicle.
   - Receive reminders tied to their vehicle (e.g., “oil change due”).
