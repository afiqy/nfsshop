** 🏭 Workshop Admin (Owner of a workshop) **
Modules specific to running their own workshop:

1. User Management (Staff & Clients)
   - Create/edit staff accounts.
   - Manage client profiles (optional if client registered themselves).
   - Assign permissions/roles.

2. Workshop Setup
   - Define services (repairs, maintenance, inspections).
   - Add inventory items (parts, tools, consumables).
   - Manage pricing, discounts, tax settings.

3. Invoices & Billing
   - Create/send invoices for clients.
   - Generate receipts, credit notes, refunds.
   - Track unpaid invoices, due reminders.

4. Jobs / Service Orders
   - Create work orders from client requests.
   - Assign to staff.
   - Track job progress and completion.

5. Scheduling & Appointments
   - Calendar for booking services.
   - Client self-booking (if enabled).
   - Reminders via email/SMS/WhatsApp.

6. Inventory Management
   - Stock in/out, supplier management.
   - Low stock alerts.
   - Purchase orders for parts.

7. Reports & Analytics (for their workshop)
   - Revenue by service, staff performance.
   - Top clients, most used parts.
   - Monthly/annual financial summaries.

8. Client Engagement
   - Send promotions or reminders.
   - Track loyalty (visits, spend).
   - Record service history per client vehicle.

9. Workshop Dashboard
   - Today’s appointments, invoices due, staff availability.
   - Quick add (invoice, job, appointment).

10. Vehicle Management
    - Create/update vehicles tied to clients.
    - Fill in details if client didn’t register.
    - See full vehicle history (invoices, jobs, notes).
    - Generate reports: most serviced cars, repeat visits, service reminders.
